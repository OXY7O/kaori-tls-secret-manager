# Code of Conduct

KAORI TLS Certificate Manager aims to be a technically rigorous, welcoming and constructive open-source community.

## Our standards

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional when discussing implementations, mistakes and differing approaches.
- Offer specific, actionable technical feedback and assume reasonable intent.
- Respect differing experience levels, accessibility needs, backgrounds and perspectives.
- Protect other contributors' privacy and never post their private contact details, credentials or internal infrastructure evidence.
- Accept maintainers' moderation decisions and keep discussions focused on the project.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, stalking, sexualized hostility, personal attacks and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other project-managed spaces, as well as public conduct while representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerning behavior privately to repository maintainers using a private contact method shown on their GitHub profiles. Do not publish personal or sensitive evidence in a public issue. If the matter is a **software vulnerability**, use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may request edits, hide or remove content, lock discussions, issue warnings or restrict participation to protect the community. Reports will be evaluated with appropriate discretion; this policy does not promise a particular response time or guaranteed confidentiality from third-party services.

## Attribution

This policy is a project-specific community code of conduct, informed by common open-source collaboration principles.
